# ShotPlot Vision

## Product Statement

ShotPlot is a digital Primary Marksmanship Instructor for the shooter, coach, and mentor.

It combines:

- authoritative shooting rules,
- marksmanship doctrine,
- digital range-book data,
- performance analysis,
- and explainable coaching.

## Core Roles

### Shooter

The shooter records:

- shots,
- calls,
- conditions,
- sight settings,
- adjustments,
- scores,
- notes,
- and results.

ShotPlot helps the shooter understand patterns and decide what to do next.

### Coach

The coach reviews:

- performance by position and stage,
- called shots versus impacts,
- adjustments,
- environmental decisions,
- consistency,
- and trends over time.

ShotPlot helps the coach identify and explain priority improvements.

### Mentor or PMI

The mentor teaches:

- fundamentals,
- positions,
- natural point of aim,
- trigger control,
- wind,
- zeroing,
- shot process,
- and match preparation.

ShotPlot connects recorded evidence to instruction and practice.
